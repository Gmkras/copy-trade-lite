- [x] 2.4 Volume histogram (design D7): a `HistogramSeries` on `priceScaleId: "volume"` with `scaleMargins: { top: 0.8, bottom: 0 }`, coloured by bar direction at 40 % opacity, added only when `variant === "full"`.   **Observed, and it corrected the design a second time (`design.md.old2` kept).** The histogram sits on its own `priceScaleId: "volume"` with `scaleMargins { top: 0.8, bottom: 0 }`. The first attempt still let the two regions overlap: the price scale's own bottom margin was 0.12, so the price series could draw down to 88 % of the pane while volume started at 80 %. Raising the price scale's bottom margin to 0.24 on full charts separates them — **price now ends at 76 %, volume starts at 80 %, no overlap** — and the price series still draws 250 px of a 420 px chart. To make "the volume did not squash the price" a measurement rather than an opinion, the chart now writes `data-price-span` (pixels between the highest high and the lowest low), the same kind of diagnostic as the existing `data-visible-bars`. Cards get no histogram: the feed showed 7 card charts at 170 px and 1 full chart at 260 px, with exactly 1 OHLC readout and 1 countdown on the page.

  **Rebuild cost, and an honest correction.** Over 120 s of steady state on `/trade` (8 candle polls) the page produced **2 long tasks, 50 ms and 59 ms**; a separate 60 s window gave 1 of 52 ms. So about a quarter of rebuilds cross the 50 ms line I set, worst case 59 ms. Going to apply the mitigation this design named — "skip the volume series when the data is unchanged" — I found it **cannot work**: the effect destroys and recreates the whole chart whenever `candles` changes, so volume's data is always new at the moment it is added, and the skip is unreachable. The only real fix is to stop rebuilding (create once, `setData` on refresh), which is an explicit Non-Goal of this change because it touches the component all three screens share. The design's risk was rewritten with these numbers and that reasoning; the hitch ships knowingly and goes in the README's "what's next" with the measurements, per task 5.1.
